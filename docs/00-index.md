# How to Use This Guide

Welcome to the Author Marketing Basics Guide. This manual walks you through
marketing a book for free, step by step, with templates you can copy, fill in,
and reuse. Nothing here requires an ad budget, a marketing degree, or sharing
your personal data online.

## Who this is for

This guide was written for Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author
and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). His book
*The Massive AI Manual* explains AI "like a friend would": plain English, step
by step, never condescending. This guide uses that same voice.

The templates work for any nonfiction or fiction book, but the examples use
*The Massive AI Manual* so you can see a complete, worked example before you
adapt it to your own book.

## How to work through it

1. **Read one document at a time.** Each takes 10–20 minutes.
2. **Fill in the template.** Copy the template into your own document and
   replace every bracketed item like `[Your Book Title]`.
3. **Use the static site.** The `site/` folder contains fillable HTML templates
   you can open in any browser, type into, and print or save locally. Nothing
   is sent to a server.
4. **Follow the order.** Start with the author bio, then the press release,
   then social posts, newsletter, ARC readers, reviews, and local events. Each
   step builds on the one before it.

## Privacy-safe by default

This guide assumes you will not publish your home address, phone number, or
private email anywhere online. Use a dedicated author email address (free from
any provider), a PO box or the library's address for events, and general
locations (e.g., "Indianapolis, IN") instead of street addresses. See
`docs/privacy-safe-marketing.md` for the full checklist.
