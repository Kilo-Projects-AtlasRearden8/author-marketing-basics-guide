# Author Bio Templates

Your author bio tells readers who you are and why they should trust you. You
need three lengths: short (one or two sentences), medium (one paragraph), and
long (two or three paragraphs). Fill in the brackets, then delete the brackets.

## Short bio (for social profiles, event introductions)

> [Name] is a [profession] turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC
> in Indiana. [He/She/They] write[s] beginner-friendly guides that explain AI
> "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

**Example (Dylan Asdale):**

> Dylan Asdale is a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale
> Dynamics, LLC in Indiana. He writes beginner-friendly guides that explain AI
> "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## Long bio (for press kits, event pages, About pages)

> [Name] is a [profession] turned AI author, educator, and founder of Asdale
> Dynamics, LLC, an Indiana company focused on beginner-friendly AI education,
> books and guides, and practical tools the LLC can publish or sell.
>
> [Name] spent [number] years as a [profession], where [relevant detail about
> communicating clearly, helping people, or solving problems under pressure].
> That experience shapes [his/her/their] writing: clear, warm, tested, and
> never condescending.
>
> Asdale Dynamics publishes books, open-source templates, and small tools for
> beginners: AI education, agents and MCP, local AI, free AI coding, GitHub,
> automation, small-business and author tools (KDP publishing, marketing,
> reselling, bookkeeping), accessible life-admin helpers (paperwork organizers,
> budgeting, low-energy productivity), healthcare-worker AI aids (education
> only, never medical advice), and fun extras such as gaming and chess tools.
>
> [Name] is the author of [*Book Title*]([book link]), a step-by-step guide to
> [topic]. Readers describe it as [one-sentence reader comment].
>
> [Name] lives in [general location, e.g., Indiana] and is available for
> library talks, podcast interviews, and local events. Contact:
> [author email address — use a dedicated address, not your personal one].

## Fill-in-the-blank template

Copy this into your own file and replace every `[bracketed item]`:

```
[Name] is a [profession] turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC
(Indiana, formed October 2026). [He/She/They] [brief career detail]. [He/She/They]
write[s] [book type] that [what makes your writing different].

[Name] is the author of [*Book Title*]([book link]), which teaches [topic]
step by step.

[Name] lives in [general location]. Contact: [dedicated author email].
```
