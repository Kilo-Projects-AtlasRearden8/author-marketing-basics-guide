# Press Release Template

A press release tells local media, blogs, and newsletters that your book is out
and why it matters. Keep it to one page. Send it to local newspapers, library
newsletters, community calendars, and niche blogs. All free.

## Template

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
[Date]

[Book Title] by [Author Name] Helps [Audience] Learn [Topic] for Free

[City], Indiana — [Author Name], a [profession] turned AI author and founder of
Asdale Dynamics, LLC, announces the release of [*Book Title*], a beginner-friendly
guide to [topic], available now on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing.

[Book Title] teaches [specific skills or topics] step by step, in plain English,
with no technical background required. [One sentence about the author's unique
perspective, e.g., "Written by a registered nurse turned AI author who learned
these tools on the job."]

"[Quote from the author about why this book matters]," said [Author Name].
"[Second sentence about who the book is for and how it helps.]"

The book is available in paperback and ebook through Amazon KDP at
[Amazon book link]. [Author Name] is available for interviews, library talks,
and local events. Contact: [dedicated author email address].

About Asdale Dynamics, LLC:
Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026) publishes beginner-friendly
AI education, books and guides, and practical tools. Focus areas include AI
education, agents and MCP, local AI, free AI coding, GitHub, automation,
small-business and author tools, accessible life-admin helpers, and healthcare-
worker AI aids (education only, never medical advice). Learn more at
https://dylanasdale.github.io

###

[Author Name]
[dedicated author email]
[general location]
[book Amazon link]
```

## Where to send it (free)

- Your local newspaper's community or books editor
- Library newsletters and event calendars in your city/county
- Community calendars at local colleges and coffee shops
- Niche blogs and newsletters in your book's topic area (AI, nursing, writing,
  self-publishing)
- Your own newsletter and social accounts

## Before you send (privacy-safe checklist)

- [ ] No home address or personal phone number — use a general location and
  dedicated author email only
- [ ] No personal data about other people (family, coworkers, patients)
- [ ] All quotes are from you and approved by you
- [ ] Book links point to public retail pages only
