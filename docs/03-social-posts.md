# Social Media Post Templates

You do not need to be on every platform. Pick one or two where your readers
already hang out, post consistently, and keep it helpful rather than salesy.
Every template below is fill-in-the-blank and free to use.

## Tone reminders

- Write like a friend explaining something useful
- Lead with value, not a sales pitch
- One call to action per post
- Never share personal data: no addresses, phone numbers, or private emails
- Use a general location ("Indiana") rather than a street address

## Ongoing post ideas

- "Tool of the week": one free AI tool and how to use it
- Myth-busting: "AI won't replace [profession] — here's what it actually does"
- Behind the scenes: a photo of your writing space (no personal items visible)
- Reader question: answer one question from comments
- Reminder: library events, local readings, newsletter sign-up

## Platform notes

- **Facebook / Instagram:** short text + image, link in bio or first comment
- **X / Threads:** one idea per post, 1–2 hashtags max (#AIForBeginners,
  #NonFiction, #IndianaAuthors)
- **LinkedIn:** longer, professional tone; good for healthcare-worker and
  small-business audiences
- **TikTok / Reels:** 30–60 second "how-to" clips; show your screen, not your
  home

## Privacy-safe rules

- [ ] Never post your home address, phone number, or personal email
- [ ] Never post photos of patients, coworkers, or private documents
- [ ] Use a general location only ("Indiana," "Indianapolis area")
- [ ] Use a dedicated author email for contact
- [ ] Get permission before quoting anyone by name; first name + last initial
  is safest
