# Newsletter Setup (Free)

A mailing list is the most reliable way to reach readers directly. You own it —
no algorithm can hide your emails from your subscribers. Everything below can be
set up for free.

## Step 1: Pick a free newsletter provider

Choose a provider with a free tier that fits your needs:

| Provider | Free tier (approx.) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Mailchimp | Up to 500 contacts, 1,000 emails/month | Beginners, simple automations |
| MailerLite | Up to 1,000 subscribers, 12,000 emails/month | Clean templates, landing pages |
| ConvertKit | Up to 10 subscribers (free plan limited) | Creators (paid upgrade sooner) |
| Substack | Unlimited subscribers (free to start) | Writers who want a public archive |

All have public privacy policies you can read before signing up. No personal
data about your readers is shared publicly — only you see the subscriber list.

> **Tip:** Start with one provider. Moving a list later is possible but adds
> steps.

## Step 2: Create your list

1. Sign up with your dedicated author email address (not your personal email).
2. Name the list something clear, like "[Book Title] Newsletter" or "[Author
   Name] — AI for Beginners."
3. Add your general location (city/state) only if required by anti-spam law —
   never your home address. Use a PO box or business address if you have one.

## Step 3: Add a sign-up form

Create a simple sign-up form and place it on:

- Your website or hub site (e.g., dylanasdale.github.io)
- Your Amazon author page bio (where links are allowed)
- The back matter of your book
- Social media profiles (link in bio)

Ask for only what you need: name (optional) and email address. The fewer fields,
the more people sign up.

## Step 4: Write your welcome email

Send it automatically when someone signs up. Template:

```
Subject: Welcome to the [Book Title] newsletter

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for signing up. I'm [Author Name], author of [*Book Title*]([book link])
and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC in Indiana.

This newsletter is free and comes about once a [month/quarter]. You'll get:
- New book and guide announcements
- Free AI tools and tutorials
- Links to local events and readings

You can unsubscribe at any time — the link is at the bottom of every email.

To start, here's [one free resource: a chapter, tool list, or reading guide]:
[link]

Happy reading,

[Author Name]
Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana)
[dedicated author email]
```

## Step 5: Send on a schedule

- Start with once a month or once a quarter. Consistency matters more than
  frequency.
- Each email: one main topic, one free resource, one call to action.
- Keep emails short. Busy readers skim.

## Step 6: Grow the list for free

- Mention the newsletter in every social post (link in bio)
- Add the sign-up link to the back matter of your book
- Offer a free reader resource (discussion questions, tool checklist, chapter
  preview) in exchange for signing up
- Mention it at local events and library talks
- Never buy an email list — it violates privacy policies and anti-spam law

## Privacy-safe checklist

- [ ] Sign up with a dedicated author email address
- [ ] No home address in the footer — use a general location, PO box, or
  business address if available
- [ ] Collect only email (and optional name)
- [ ] Every email has a working unsubscribe link
- [ ] Never share or sell the subscriber list
- [ ] Read your provider's privacy policy before uploading any list
