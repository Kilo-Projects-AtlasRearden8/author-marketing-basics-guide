# ARC Readers (Advance Reader Copies)

ARC readers read your book before publication and leave honest reviews when it
launches. They are one of the best free ways to build early buzz. You do not
need to pay them — a free copy and a thank-you are the standard arrangement.

## What an ARC reader does

- Reads your book before the public launch date
- Posts an honest review on release day (Amazon, Goodreads, StoryGraph, blogs)
- Tells friends who might enjoy the book

## Who to recruit (free)

- Friends and acquaintances who read in your genre (nonfiction, AI, nursing,
  self-publishing, beginner tech guides)
- Followers from your social accounts and newsletter
- Local library patrons and book club members
- Online communities in your topic area (Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups)
  — follow each community's self-promotion rules
- Other beginner-friendly authors who swap ARCs

## How to recruit (template)

```
Subject: ARC reader invitation — [Book Title]

Hi [Name],

I'm [Author Name], author of [*Book Title*]([book link]), a beginner-friendly
guide to [topic] being published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana).

I'm looking for a small group of ARC (advance reader) readers to read the book
before launch and post honest reviews on release day. You'd receive a free
digital copy and my thanks in the book's acknowledgments (optional).

No obligation to give a positive review — I only ask for an honest one.

If you're interested, reply with your preferred format (PDF, EPUB, or Kindle)
and I'll send a copy.

Thank you,

[Author Name]
[dedicated author email]
```

## Sending copies safely

- Use PDF, EPUB, or Kindle "Send to Kindle" — all free
- Do not include DRM that prevents legitimate reading
- Watermarking is optional; a simple "Advance Reader Copy — not for resale"
  notice on the title page is enough
- Keep a private spreadsheet of who received a copy (name/email only, stored
  locally, never published online)
- Ask reviewers to post within [number] days of launch

## Review request guidelines (what you can and cannot say)

**OK to say:**
- "I'd appreciate an honest review when the book launches."
- "Reviews help more than any ad budget."
- "Post on Amazon, Goodreads, StoryGraph, or your blog — wherever you read."

**Never say or imply:**
- "Only post if you liked it."
- "I'll give you [gift card/free book] for a five-star review."
- "Post the same review I wrote for you."

Paid or incentivized positive reviews violate retailer policies and damage
trust. Honest reviews only.

## Privacy-safe checklist

- [ ] No home addresses shared with ARC readers
- [ ] Reader contact info stored locally, never posted online
- [ ] Reviews are honest and unpaid
- [ ] First name + last initial only when quoting readers publicly
- [ ] Get permission before quoting anyone by name
