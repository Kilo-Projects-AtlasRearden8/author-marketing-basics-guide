# Local Events and Readings

Local events cost nothing but time and build real connections with readers.
Libraries, bookstores, coffee shops, and community centers often host local
authors for free.

## Event types

| Event | What happens | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Book reading | Read a chapter aloud, Q&A | Nonfiction and memoir |
| Library talk | Teach one topic from your book (30–45 min) | AI guides, how-to books |
| Book signing | Sign copies, meet readers | Launch week |
| Workshop | Hands-on practice with a tool from your book | Practical/technical books |
| Panel discussion | Join local authors on a topic | Writing and publishing groups |

## Finding venues (free)

- Public libraries — contact the events or programming coordinator
- Independent bookstores — ask about local author events
- Community centers, churches, senior centers
- Coffee shops with event space
- Local colleges and nursing schools (for healthcare topics)

## Outreach email template

```
Subject: Local author event: [Book Title] at [Venue]

Dear [Coordinator name],

My name is [Author Name]. I'm a [profession] turned AI author and founder of
Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana). I've written [*Book Title*]([book link]), a
beginner-friendly guide to [topic].

I'd like to host a free [reading / talk / workshop] at [Venue]. The event would
run about [30–60 minutes], include a short reading or demo, and end with Q&A.
I bring my own materials and handle promotion through my newsletter and social
accounts.

Suggested dates: [date 1], [date 2], or [date 3].

Would any of these work for you?

Thank you,

[Author Name]
[dedicated author email]
[general location]
[book link]
```

## Planning checklist (4–6 weeks ahead)

- [ ] Confirm date, time, and room with the venue
- [ ] Confirm whether you can sell/sign books (some venues ask for a
  percentage or don't allow sales — plan around it)
- [ ] Plan the format: welcome (5 min), reading/demo (20–30 min), Q&A
  (10–15 min), signing/social time (10 min)
- [ ] Create a one-page flyer: title, date, time, venue, your general location,
  dedicated author email, book cover image
- [ ] Post the event on the venue's calendar, your social accounts, and
  community calendars (library, city, colleges)
- [ ] Announce it in your newsletter
- [ ] Bring: books, sign-up sheet for your newsletter (email only), pen, phone
  for photos (ask permission before photographing attendees)
- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early to test any tech

## Privacy-safe event rules

- [ ] Use a general location ("Indianapolis Public Library") — never your home
  address
- [ ] Use a dedicated author email for event contact
- [ ] No attendee photos without permission; if you post photos, no faces or
  names without consent
- [ ] Newsletter sign-up sheet collects email only, stored locally, never
  published online

## After the event

- [ ] Thank the venue coordinator by email
- [ ] Add new newsletter sign-ups to your list (email only)
- [ ] Post a thank-you on social media (no attendee names or faces without
  permission)
- [ ] Note what worked and what to change for next time
