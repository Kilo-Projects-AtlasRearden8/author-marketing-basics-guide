# Privacy-Safe Marketing Checklist

This guide is designed so you never need to share personal data online. Use
this checklist before publishing anything: a bio, press release, social post,
newsletter, event flyer, or website page.

## Personal data to keep offline

- [ ] Home address — use a general location (city/state) or PO box only
- [ ] Personal phone number — use a dedicated author email instead
- [ ] Personal email — use a dedicated author email address for all public
  contact
- [ ] Workplace details that identify patients, coworkers, or your employer
- [ ] Patient information of any kind (healthcare workers: never share details
  that could identify a patient)
- [ ] Family members' names or photos without permission
- [ ] IDs, documents, or financial information

## Before you publish anything

- [ ] No street addresses — general location only
- [ ] Contact is a dedicated author email address, not a personal one
- [ ] No photos of people without permission; no faces or names in event photos
  without consent
- [ ] Quotes from readers used only with permission (first name + last initial)
- [ ] Newsletter sign-up collects email only; list stored locally, never
  published
- [ ] ARC reader contact info stored locally, never published
- [ ] All reviews honest and unpaid

## Defaults for this guide

- Dedicated author email address for all public contact
- General location (e.g., "Indiana," "Indianapolis area") instead of addresses
- Local-first: drafts, templates, and reader lists live on your own computer
- No ad budget, no data broker services, no tracking pixels required

If something in this guide asks for information you're not comfortable sharing
publicly, leave it out or use a placeholder. The templates still work.
