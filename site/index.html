How to use this site

  1. Open a template page and fill in the bracketed fields with your own details.
  2. Click Copy to copy the finished text to your clipboard.
  3. Paste it into your own document, email, or post. Nothing is sent to a server — everything stays in your browser.
  4. Work through the guides in order: bio → press release → social → newsletter → ARC → reviews → events.
