Local Events and Readings

Generate an outreach email for libraries, bookstores, coffee shops, and community centers. Free events, real connections.

Event types

  • Book reading — read a chapter aloud, Q&A
  • Library talk — teach one topic from your book (30–45 min)
  • Book signing — sign copies, meet readers
  • Workshop — hands-on practice with a tool from your book
  • Panel discussion — join local authors on a topic

Privacy: use a general location and a dedicated author email for event contact. No attendee photos or names without permission. Newsletter sign-up at events collects email only, stored locally.
