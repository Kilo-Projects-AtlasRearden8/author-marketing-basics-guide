Newsletter Setup (Free)

Fill in your details to generate a welcome email. Copy it into your newsletter provider's automation. Everything stays in your browser.

Setup steps

  1. Pick a free provider (Mailchimp, MailerLite, or Substack) and read its privacy policy first.
  2. Sign up with your dedicated author email address — not your personal email.
  3. Add only a general location (city/state), never a home address.
  4. Place the sign-up link on your website, social profiles, and book back matter.
  5. Ask for email only (name optional). Send on a schedule: once a month or once a quarter.

Privacy: every email needs a working unsubscribe link. Never share or sell the subscriber list. Never buy an email list.
