The Massive AI Manual by Dylan Asdale Helps Beginners Learn AI Step by Step

Indianapolis, Indiana — Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC, announces the release of *The Massive AI Manual*, a beginner-friendly guide to practical AI skills, available now on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing.

The Massive AI Manual teaches AI tools, agents and MCP, local AI, free AI coding, GitHub, and automation — explained "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. Written by a registered nurse who learned these tools on the job, the book assumes no technical background.

"I wrote this book for anyone who has ever felt left behind by AI," said Dylan Asdale. "You don't need to code, spend money on ads, or have a computer science degree. You just need a friend who will show you the steps."

The book is available in paperback and ebook through Amazon KDP at https://amazon.com/dp/[ASIN]. Dylan Asdale is available for interviews, library talks, and local events. Contact: dylan@example-indiana-author.com.
