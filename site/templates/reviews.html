Gathering Book Reviews

Generate review request language for your newsletter and book back matter. Honest reviews only.

Where reviews matter most

  • Amazon — most visible retail reviews
  • Goodreads / StoryGraph — discovery and reader communities
  • Your website or hub site — pull quotes you have permission to use
  • Library and bookseller catalogs — reader suggestions

Never: pay for reviews, offer gifts for positive reviews, ask family members, write your own reviews under different names, or trade reviews. Always ask permission before quoting a reader; first name + last initial only.
